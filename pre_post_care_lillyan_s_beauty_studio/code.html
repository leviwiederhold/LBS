Care Guide

Our commitment to your beauty extends beyond our studio. Proper preparation and aftercare ensure the best possible results and lasting radiance.

spa

Facials

Pre-Care

  • • Avoid sun exposure 48 hours prior.
  • • Discontinue Retin-A or AHAs/BHAs 5 days before.
  • • Arrive with a clean, makeup-free face if possible.

Post-Care

  • • Stay hydrated and avoid heavy makeup for 24 hours.
  • • Apply SPF 30+ daily to protect your fresh skin.
  • • Avoid intense heat, saunas, or steam for 48 hours.
water_drop

Waxing

Pre-Care

  • • Hair should be at least 1/4 inch long (grain of rice).
  • • Gently exfoliate the area 24 hours before your visit.
  • • Wear loose-fitting clothing for your comfort.

Post-Care

  • • Avoid hot baths, swimming, and exercise for 24 hours.
  • • Wait 48 hours before exfoliating the treated area.
  • • Apply a soothing, fragrance-free moisturizer.
face

Permanent Makeup

Pre-Care

  • • No caffeine, alcohol, or ibuprofen 24 hours before.
  • • Avoid blood thinners and fish oil for one week prior.
  • • No chemical peels or Botox 4 weeks before treatment.

Post-Care

  • • Keep the area dry and clean; do not pick at scabs.
  • • Apply the provided healing balm as instructed.
  • • Avoid direct sun and swimming until fully healed.
brush

Formal & Wedding

Pre-Care

  • • Exfoliate and hydrate your skin the night before.
  • • Wear a button-down shirt to avoid messing up hair/makeup.
  • • Have inspiration photos ready for your artist.

Post-Care

  • • Use a setting spray for extended wear.
  • • Keep a touch-up kit (lipstick, blotting papers) handy.
  • • Double cleanse at night to remove all long-wear products.
